Certifications & Quality

Quality Is Our DNA — Engineered, Tested, and Certified to Global Standards

At Isler, quality is not a checkpoint — it is built into every stage of our process. We follow a zero-defect philosophy, backed by a robust Quality Management System (QMS), advanced in-house testing labs, and rigorous compliance with national and international standards.

Beyond the Standard

Isler conducts twice as many quality tests as current BIS suggested standards require — across both our Chimney and Hobs/Cooktops categories — reinforcing our commitment to consistent, reliable product performance well beyond minimum compliance.

ISO Certifications

ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management System, implemented with external experts and covering clearly defined processes, SOPs, and templates across every function.

ISO 14001:2015 — Environmental Management System, ensuring our manufacturing operations are managed responsibly and sustainably.

BIS Licenses

Isler Infra India Private Limited holds active Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) licenses, certifying our products meet national quality and safety standards.

Quality Infrastructure

Advanced testing and quality labs for incoming, in-line, and outgoing quality checks (IQC, LQC, OQC).

Pre-dispatch inspection (PDI) on every batch before it leaves our facility.

Real-time quality data captured and tracked through our ERP and Supply Network Control Tower, giving partners full visibility into product quality at every stage.
