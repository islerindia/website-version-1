Manufacturing

A State-of-the-Art Facility Built for Scale, Speed, and Precision

Isler’s manufacturing facility in Greater Noida was inaugurated by Shri Sanjiv Singh, Joint Secretary, DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce and Industry — an event covered by more than 30 media channels. Isler was also featured by the Hon’ble Union Minister of Commerce & Industry, Mr. Piyush Goyal, in recognition of its contribution to India’s manufacturing journey.

Infrastructure at a Glance

30,000 sq. ft.
Assembly, Warehousing, Quality Labs, NPD
Integrated Facility

Our integrated facility brings assembly lines, warehousing, advanced quality labs, and new product development (NPD) capabilities under one roof — enabling faster iteration from design to dispatch.

Process-Led, Digital & Quality-First Operations

World-Class Manufacturing Organization — a structured, role-driven organization with KPI-led digital manufacturing, built to global standards.

Process Excellence — QMS-led operations, ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 compliant, with standardized workflows across 13+ processes, 25+ sub-processes, and 130+ templates.

ERP & Data Backbone — end-to-end operations on Odoo ERP, covering everything from BOM definition and purchase orders to quality checks, dispatch, and invoicing, with real-time data capture and visibility.

Supply Network Control Tower (SNCT) — a trigger-based system for real-time exception detection, root cause analysis, and corrective action tracking across the supply chain — giving our partners full visibility from plan to delivery.

Digital Shopfloor — a live shopfloor control tower tracking manpower, work-in-progress, and dispatch status in real time, supported by standardized digital work instructions for consistent, skill-independent execution.

Scaling for the Future

We are continuing to expand our backward integration capabilities and manufacturing capacity to meet growing global demand — building toward a long-term ambition of 1 million units per year, with planned investment in additional facilities and deeper localization of components across our product range.

Innovation. Speed. Trust. That’s Isler.
