Dough Makers

Automatic dough makers for global brands – 5L aluminium non-stick bowls with dual handles, 850W of power (650W heating + 200W mixing), 40–100°C fermentation and 0–30 min timing. Touch-panel or knob controls, a glass cover and a detachable pot; kneads, heats and ferments dough and makes FUFU – all customizable for bulk and private-label orders.
